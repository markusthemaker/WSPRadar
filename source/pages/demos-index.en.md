<!-- text: shared.demo_context -->
These demos draw on published WSPR studies, comparing reported findings with WSPRadar analyses of the available observations. They demonstrate station comparisons, calibration, Performance analysis, and tracing results back to individual reports, while explaining differences in methods, presentation, and data coverage.
