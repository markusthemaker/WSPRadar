<!-- text: landing.what_title -->
What is WSPRadar?
<!-- /text -->

<!-- text: landing.what_body -->
WSPRadar is a free web tool that analyzes public <strong>WSPR reception reports</strong> to help you understand and compare your station’s on-air performance. <strong>It does not transmit or receive radio signals — it uses data that stations around the world have already uploaded.</strong>

<!-- text: landing.operating_note.intro -->
Choose a period when both setups are known to be operating on the selected band.

TX: both transmitters must transmit in the same WSPR cycles.
<!-- /text -->

<!-- text: landing.operating_note.detail -->
