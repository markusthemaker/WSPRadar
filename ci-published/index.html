What is WSPRadar?

WSPRadar is a free web tool that analyzes public WSPR reception reports to help you understand and compare your station’s on-air performance. It does not transmit or receive radio signals — it uses data that stations around the world have already uploaded. Your station can be part of this too — use past and newly uploaded reports to compare antenna setups, evaluate improvements, and make better-informed decisions about your station.

RX Benchmark

Which of my receive setups hears better?

Compare two receive chains, such as different antennas or preamps. Use ΔSNR to compare matched signal quality and ΔDecode to compare what each setup decodes.

TX Benchmark

Which transmit setup is heard better?

Compare two transmitters using reports from the same receivers in the same WSPR cycles. Use ΔSNR to compare matched signal quality and ΔDecode to compare which transmissions get decoded.

Before you benchmark

Choose a period when both setups are known to be operating on the selected band.

  • RX: both receivers must be listening and reporting throughout the comparison period.
  • TX: both transmitters must transmit in the same WSPR cycles.

Reception reports alone cannot verify continuous operation. Outages or missing uploads can resemble missed decodes.
