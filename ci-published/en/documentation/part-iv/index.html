Part II: Practical Setup Guide

Set up two receive paths, prepare a simultaneous transmit comparison, or measure an RX correction. Choose the route for your experiment and complete its checks before recording the measurement data.

Goal Route Ready when
Simultaneous RX on Windows Chapter 4: create, optionally clone, then verify the two WSJT-X paths. Continue to Chapter 6 if calibration is needed. Both receivers upload under the expected callsigns and locators, with correct audio/data paths and synchronized clocks.
Controlled simultaneous TX Chapter 5: plan the setup, follow the hardware recipe, then check timing and signals, power and database reports. Use 5.6 to confirm the result. Common receivers report both clean, correctly placed signals in the intended cycles, under the expected callsigns and locators.
Documented RX SNR correction Chapter 6: common-input measurement, consistency checks, signed correction and independent validation. The offset is stable within recorded conditions and supported by separate validation evidence.

Use instructions for the exact hardware and firmware; the ZachTek recipe uses standard scheduling and frequency randomization. When ready, return to Section 1.2. Keep No correction unless an offset is justified.
